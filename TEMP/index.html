Keyboard & Mouse Repair Questions

How ordering, shipping and payment work when you send in a device from anywhere in India.

Do you provide keyboard repair across India?+

Yes. KeebForge is a mail-in service based in Jammu & Kashmir and accepts keyboards from anywhere in India. Ship your keyboard to us, we repair, modify or build it, and ship it back.

Can I ship my keyboard to KeebForge?+

Yes. Place an order on the order page, confirm the details, then ship your keyboard to Jammu & Kashmir. The buyer covers shipping in both directions.

How do I send my keyboard or mouse to KeebForge?+

Place an order on the order page, confirm the details and payment on Discord or email, then ship your device to Jammu & Kashmir and share the tracking details.

Do you ship the repaired keyboard back?+

Yes. Once the work is complete and tested, your keyboard or mouse is securely shipped back to you anywhere in India.

Can I use KeebForge if I'm outside your city?+

Yes. There is no physical walk-in shop — KeebForge is a mail-in service that accepts work from anywhere in India and ships it back.

Do you repair mechanical keyboards?+

Yes. Dead keys, rattling stabilizers, scratchy switches, damaged hot-swap sockets and PCB faults are all repaired at component level.

Do you repair gaming mice?+

Yes — double-clicking switches, dead scroll wheels (encoder replacement), worn skates, and cable, sensor or connectivity issues.
